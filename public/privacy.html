Privacy Policy

Barsmith · Effective 5 August 2026

Barsmith has no account, no login, and no server that holds your writing.

Every bar you write, every word you save, and your whole session history live in your browser's storage on your own device. They are never uploaded, never backed up to a cloud, and never seen by anyone — including me.

The app sends anonymous usage counts so I can tell which features earn their place. Those counts never contain anything you typed, and you can switch them off in Settings → Privacy.

That is the whole policy in three sentences. The rest of this page is the detail behind it, because a promise about privacy is worth exactly as much as its specifics.

1. What stays on your device

All of this is stored using your browser's localStorage on the device you are using. It is not transmitted anywhere:

  • Your bars — everything written in the Bar Pad, including drafts recovered after a crash
  • Saved words and any personal vocabulary you add
  • Session history — the words you were given, how long you wrote, what you wrote
  • Lifetime totals, practice days and streaks
  • Your settings — level, scheme, tempo, haptics, metronome volume, and your analytics choice

There is no copy of any of it anywhere else. That is the point of the design, and it has a consequence worth stating plainly: if you delete the app, clear your browser's site data, or lose the device, that writing is gone. I cannot recover it, because I never had it. Use Saved Words → Export Backup to keep a copy you control.

2. What is sent, and what it contains

Barsmith records anonymous, aggregated usage events through Vercel Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, assigns you no identifier, and does not follow you to any other site.

These are all of the events the app is capable of sending. There are no others:

EventSent when
app_openThe app is opened
session_startA writing session begins
session_endA writing session finishes
daily_completeThe daily prescribed session is completed
progress_openThe Progress screen is opened
rhyme_searchA rhyme search is run — never the word searched for
word_lockA word is tapped mid-session — never which word
bar_shareA bar card is shared
exportAn export or backup is produced

The properties attached to them are bucketed ranges, never exact figures

A session reports 6-15 bars rather than 12, and 5-10 minutes rather than 7. An exact count paired with a timestamp begins to look like a fingerprint, and nothing here needs that resolution. The same applies to session counts, day counts and streak lengths.

Content cannot get in, by construction

This is not a policy promise resting on care — it is enforced in the code. Event properties are filtered against a pattern permitting only short lowercase labels and small numbers, and anything failing that test is dropped rather than sent. There is no path through which a bar, a saved word, a search term or a filename could travel, even by mistake.

Do Not Track is honoured

If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, nothing is sent, whatever the in-app setting says.

3. The one exception: word lookups

Rhymes, syllable counts, stress patterns and the built-in word definitions all run entirely on your device — the dictionaries ship inside the app, which is why they keep working in airplane mode.

Tapping a word for its extended definition and synonyms is the exception. That sends the single word — and nothing else — to two public dictionary services:

  • api.dictionaryapi.dev — definitions
  • api.datamuse.com — synonyms, antonyms and related words

It happens only at the moment you tap, only for that one word, and it carries no identifier of any kind. Those services have their own privacy practices, which I do not control. If you never tap a word for synonyms, nothing is ever sent to either.

4. Camera, microphone and audio

Barsmith can record you rapping. If you use it, your device will ask for camera and microphone permission first, and you can refuse — the rest of the app works normally either way.

Recordings are never uploaded. The video is produced and held in your device's memory, played back in the app if you want to review it, and discarded when the session ends unless you choose to save it. Saving hands the file to your operating system's own share sheet, at which point where it goes is entirely your choice. No recording is transmitted to me or to anyone else at any stage.

The same is true of any instrumental you load: the audio file is read locally and never leaves the device.

5. What Barsmith never does

  • No accounts, no email addresses, no phone numbers, no names
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no third-party trackers, no social media pixels
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is nothing personal to sell
  • No cookies
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, or files beyond one instrumental you explicitly choose to load
  • No cross-site or cross-app tracking, and no data broker relationships

6. Your controls

  • Turn analytics off — Settings → Privacy. It takes effect on the very next event, not after a restart. Every other part of the app behaves identically.
  • Export everything — Saved Words → Export Backup produces a JSON file containing all your writing, which you keep.
  • Delete everything — clearing the site data in your browser settings, or deleting the app from your home screen, removes all of it permanently and immediately. There is no server-side deletion request to make, because there is no server-side copy.

7. Legal basis and your rights

Where the UK GDPR, EU GDPR or similar law applies, the anonymous usage counts are processed on the basis of legitimate interest in understanding whether the app's features work, and you may object at any time using the in-app switch. No personal data as those laws define it is collected, so there is in practice nothing to request access to, correct, or port — everything Barsmith holds about you is already on your own device and exportable in one tap.

Under the CCPA and similar US state laws: Barsmith does not sell or share personal information, and has never done so.

8. Children

Barsmith is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect anything from them. Since it collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing held about a child user to delete — but if you have a concern, get in touch.

9. Changes to this policy

If what the app sends ever changes, this page changes with it and the effective date at the top is updated. A change that broadened what leaves your device would be announced in the app itself, not quietly published here.

10. Contact

Questions about any of this, or about your data: hello@barsmith.app

A real person reads it — there is no support desk behind this, and no ticket number coming back.
