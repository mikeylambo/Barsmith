# Barsmith — Beta

Thanks for giving this your time. It's a writing gym: a word lands on the screen, you
build a bar around it, and it keeps a record so you can see yourself getting sharper.
No account, no feed, nothing to scroll.

**You're testing it because I want to know whether it's worth keeping, not whether it
works.** It works. The question is whether you'd actually come back to it.

---

## 1. Install it (iPhone)

It has to be **Safari** — not Chrome. Apple only lets Safari install web apps.

1. Open **barsmith.vercel.app** in Safari
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow, at the bottom)
3. Scroll down → **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**
5. **Launch it from the home-screen icon**, not the Safari tab

That last step matters. From the icon it runs full-screen, works offline, and behaves
like an app. From a Safari tab it's fine but slower and it can't go offline properly.

**Android:** same idea in Chrome — you'll usually get an "Install app" prompt, or it's
under the ⋮ menu → *Add to Home screen*.

---

## 2. Read this before you write anything real

**Your bars live on your phone and nowhere else.** There's no cloud, no account, no
server holding a copy. That's deliberate — your unreleased writing isn't sitting on
someone else's computer.

The flip side: **if you delete the app or clear Safari's data, your work is gone.** I
can't recover it, because I never had it.

So once you've written something you'd be annoyed to lose:

> **Vault → Export Backup** → save the `.json` somewhere (Files, Drive, email it to
> yourself). *Restore Backup* on the same screen brings it all back.

Do this once a week. Takes five seconds. I'd rather nag you now than apologise later.

You can also get bars out as plain text any time — *Export .txt* on the Summary screen
after a session, or *Export All Bars* from History.

## 3. What to actually do

No script. Use it how you'd use it. But if you want a shape:

**Days 1–3 — just play.** Hit *Start Session* and see what happens. Try tapping a word
mid-session (that locks it and pulls up its rhymes and meaning). Change Level and
Scheme and feel the difference.

**Days 4–10 — take the daily.** The card at the top of the home screen prescribes a
session — level, pace, length, all chosen. Different shape every day of the week:
Monday's Foundations, Friday's Sprint, Sunday's Endurance. Just tap it and go.
**This is the part I most need judged.** It exists so you don't have to make five
decisions before you can write.

**Days 10+ — check Progress.** Tap the flame, or the Progress button. Bars written,
time trained, vocabulary breadth, a year-long consistency grid. Tell me whether any of
it makes you want to keep the streak alive, or whether it's just numbers.

**At some point, try:**
- **Rhyme Search** (the ◎ button) — type any word, including one you think has no
  rhyme. `orange`, `silver`, `month`, `purple`. It runs entirely on your phone.
- **Your Words** — Vault → add your own vocabulary. Names, slang, places. They start
  showing up in your sessions.
- **Airplane mode.** Turn it on and open the app. Everything except word *definitions*
  should still work — rhymes, sessions, the lot.
- **Sharing a bar.** Summary or History → the share icon on any bar → it makes an image.

## 4. What I need back

Generic feedback doesn't help much. These do:

**The words**
- Which Level feels most useful? Does Level 3 feel like a challenge, or like a
  vocabulary test?
- Any word that made you stop and think *"nobody raps with that"*?
- Any word that gave you a bar you actually liked? Those are the ones I want more of.

**Coming back**
- Did you open it on a day nobody reminded you to? What made you?
- If you stopped, **when and why?** This is the single most useful thing you can tell
  me, and it's the one people don't say because it feels rude. It isn't. "Forgot about
  it after day 3" is worth more than a paragraph of praise.

**The daily session**
- Did it feel like a workout, or a chore?
- Did you ever ignore it and set your own settings instead? Why?

**The rhymes**
- Anything flat wrong? The engine is new and I tuned it by hand — I want to know if it
  tells you two words rhyme when your ear says otherwise.

**Anything broken**
- Did you lose any writing? **Tell me immediately**, that's the one bug I treat as
  critical.
- Anything covered up by the keyboard, cut off by the notch, or unreadable in sunlight.

## 5. Known rough edges — don't waste time reporting these

- **Word definitions need a connection.** Rhymes, syllables and stress are all on your
  phone; the dictionary definition is the one thing that still needs a signal.
- **No beats yet.** BPM mode works and you can load your own instrumental, but nothing
  ships with it. That's coming.
- **No notifications, no reminders.** Deliberate for now — I want to know if you come
  back *without* being nudged.
- **No sync between devices.** Backup file only.
- **It's a web app, not in the App Store.** That's the next step if this proves out.

---

## 6. What gets sent

**Nothing you write.** Not a bar, not a vault word, not a rhyme search. Ever.

Barsmith does send anonymous counts — that a session happened, roughly how long,
roughly how many bars, whether it came from the daily card. No account, no cookies,
nothing that ties it to you. It's there so I can tell which parts of this earn their
place instead of guessing.

Turn it off any time: **Settings → Privacy** (the ⚙ at the top of the home screen). Everything else works identically.

One exception, so you know: tapping a word for its *synonyms* sends that single word to
a public dictionary. Just the word, only when you tap.
